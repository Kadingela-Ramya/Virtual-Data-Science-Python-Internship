# Week 2: Advanced Data Visualization and Storytelling with Python

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is part of my **Virtual Data Science with Python Apprentice Internship**.

The objective of Week 2 is to explore advanced data visualization techniques and use data storytelling to communicate meaningful insights from a dataset to a non-technical audience.

For this project, I analyzed the **World Happiness Report 2024** dataset to understand how different economic, social, and well-being factors are associated with happiness across countries.

---

## 📊 Dataset

**Dataset:** World Happiness Report 2024

The dataset includes information about:

- Country Name
- Regional Indicator
- Happiness Score (Ladder Score)
- GDP per Capita
- Social Support
- Healthy Life Expectancy
- Freedom to Make Life Choices
- Generosity
- Perceptions of Corruption

---

## 🎯 Objective

The main objective of this project is to create meaningful visualizations and develop a clear data story around the question:

> **What factors are most strongly associated with happiness across countries?**

---

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- Python
- Google Colab
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Plotly

---

## 📈 Visualizations Created

The following visualizations were created:

1. Distribution of Happiness Scores
2. Top 10 Happiest Countries
3. Average Happiness Score by Region
4. Relationship Between GDP and Happiness
5. Relationship Between Social Support and Happiness
6. Correlation Heatmap
7. Interactive Visualization of GDP, Social Support, and Happiness

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Social Support showed the strongest meaningful relationship with happiness, with a correlation of approximately **0.81**.
- Log GDP per Capita showed a strong positive correlation of approximately **0.77**.
- Healthy Life Expectancy also showed a strong positive relationship with happiness, with a correlation of approximately **0.76**.
- Freedom to Make Life Choices had a moderate positive correlation of approximately **0.64**.
- Generosity showed the weakest relationship with happiness among the analyzed factors.

These findings suggest that happiness is influenced by multiple factors and is not determined by economic prosperity alone. Social support, health, and personal freedom also appear to play important roles.
